# Opinion fidelity fixes — 2026-09-04

Branch: `mwilliams/opinion-fidelity-fixes-2026-09-04` (off `master` @ `3cfd053`)

These are the **clear implementation errors** from the SMTCA opinion fidelity
review (all 46 published opinions vs. their canonical adopted PDFs). Only fixes
where the canonical settles the correct value unambiguously are included.
Everything requiring a judgment call, missing source text, or a schema/generator
change was **deliberately left alone** — see "Not fixed" below.

Ledger: `platform/partners/oll/partners/us/nsn/mohican/council/tests/ledger/opinion/`

**18 files changed · 21 fixes across 9 finding classes.** Every changed file was
re-parsed for well-formedness, the ¶ sequence re-verified as `1..N` for all 46
opinions, and all 46 DOCX regenerated (0 failures) with the corrections visible
in the rendered output.

---

## 1. Altered term in an operative order (D-15)

**2013 SMTCA 3** (`2013/3.xml`) — the single dispositive sentence of the ORDER
read "unused or forfeited **FTO**" while the same sentence said "320 **PTO**
hours".

- **Fixed:** `FTO` → `PTO`.
- **Canonical:** p.5 ORDER reads "unused or forfeited **PTO** and a bank of 320
  PTO hours" — read directly from the page image.
- **Note:** the internal inconsistency inside one sentence was the tell. The
  whole appeal concerns paid time off.

## 2. Altered words inside quotations of law (D-17)

Each was cross-checked against the *same opinion's own* correct quotation of the
same passage, then against the canonical.

| File | Was | Now | Canonical |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2010/2.xml` ¶23 | "shall not **he** heard" | "shall not **be** heard" | its own ¶12 quotes it correctly |
| `2015/1.xml` ¶8 | "supervisor**. If** applicable" | "supervisor**, if** applicable" | p.2 block quote, verified on the page |
| `2012/1.xml` ¶9 | "Section 1.13(**G(**5)(c)" | "Section 1.13(**G)(**5)(c)" | its own ¶4 and ¶13 are correct |
| `2002/1.xml` | "requires a **libera** interpretation" | "requires a **liberal** interpretation" | p.6 line 11 |

The 2015 fix matters most: turning a trailing conditional clause into a separate
sentence changed the pleading requirement the court was quoting.

## 3. Character-level transcription errors (D-13)

- **`2002/2.xml`** — "Ma​y 06, 2002" contained **two Cyrillic homoglyphs**,
  `М` (U+041C CYRILLIC CAPITAL EM) and `а` (U+0430 CYRILLIC SMALL A). Both
  replaced with ASCII. *The review reported one; there were two.* Invisible on
  the page but breaks text search and collation.
- **`2011/1.xml`** ¶2 — cite text read `$23 (C)`; now `§23 (C)`.
- **`2015/1.xml`** — **found by sweeping for the family, not in the original
  report**: `$53.3(M)` where the identical cite in the same sentence used
  `§53.3(M)`. Canonical p.2 reads `§53.3(M)` throughout.

A corpus-wide sweep for Cyrillic/Greek/fullwidth homoglyphs and for `$` before a
digit in cite text now returns **zero** hits.

## 4. Truncated party name (D-07)

**2008 SMTCA 2** (`2008/2.xml`) — appellee `<name>` stopped mid-phrase at
"Stockbridge-Munsee Tribal".

- **Fixed:** → "Stockbridge-Munsee Tribal Council, Enrollment Committee
  Director-Manager".
- **Canonical:** p.1 caption, read directly.
- **Note:** the document `<heading>` was already correct; only the `<party>`
  record was damaged. The plural designations ("Plaintiffs, Appellants" /
  "Defendants-Appellees") match the canonical and were **not** touched.

## 5. Effective dates contradicting the opinions' own signature blocks (D-16)

| File | Was | Now | Canonical signature block |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2021/4.xml` | `2021-08-21` | `2021-08-31` | "Entered this **31st** day of August, 2021" (p.4) |
| `2014/2.xml` | `2014-04-24` | `2014-04-15` | "Dated this April **15th**, 2014" (p.5) |

In both cases the file's own `procedural-data` already carried the correct date,
so the metadata contradicted the document body.

**Downstream:** the 2021 error is what produced the fabricated citation date
"August 21th, 2021" in 2022 SMTCA 1 ¶3 and 2023 SMTCA 1 ¶4 (D-22). Those two
citation *strings* are separate authored text and are **not** corrected here —
see "Not fixed".

## 6. Wrong reporter self-citation (D-18)

**2016 SMTCA 6** (`2016/6.xml`) — reporter citation text *and* `url` both said
`2016 SMTCA 5`, colliding with 2016 SMTCA 5's own citation.

- **Fixed:** → `<citation type="reporter" url="./2016 SMTCA 6.pdf">2016 SMTCA 6</citation>`
- **Corroboration:** canonical is Case No. AP-2016-RO-0009 (*Doug Huck, et al. v.
  SM Recall Election Committee*); `reconciled_case_numbers.json` maps
  `AP-2016-RO-0009` → `2016 SMTCA 6`.
- **Note:** `opinion.xslt` prints the reporter from `@id`, so the site header was
  already right; the defect was corrupting the `hist`/`short` citation templates
  in `docs/opinion/model.py`, which interpolate `{{citations.reporter}}`.
- Reporter-citation text now matches `id` in **46 of 46**.
- The `url` still points at a non-existent PDF — that is D-24, a policy question,
  left alone.

## 7. Case number short one digit (D-23)

- **Canonical:** p.1 caption "Appeal No. **2025-AA-0001**", repeated in the body

## 9. Unresolved citation targets — empty `doc=""` (D-09)

Six citations to the Tribal Court Rules of Procedure carried a `path` but an
empty `doc=""`, so they could not resolve.

- **Fixed:** `doc=""` → `doc="SMRP"` in `2003/1.xml`, `2006/1.xml`,
  `2010/2.xml`, `2011/1.xml` (×2), `2012/1.xml`.
- **Basis:** of the Rules-of-Procedure cites in this corpus, **9 already used
  `doc="SMRP"`** and exactly these 6 were blank — an unambiguous house
  convention, not a guess.
- Empty `doc=""` corpus-wide: **6 → 0**.
- **Note:** `2010/2.xml` also has `path="23|(1)"` without the `§` its siblings
  use. Left as-is — the path convention is a separate question, and the
  `proof="needed"` markers on these cites are retained deliberately (see below).

## 10. Split citations pointing at the wrong provision (D-04)

**2013 SMTCA 1** (`2013/1.xml`) — three citations were torn across two adjacent
`<cite>` elements whose second half dropped the section prefix, so the link
resolved to a **different section** than the visible text named.

| Visible text | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| "§ 46.6-10(A)(2)" | `path="§46.10\|(A)\|(2)"` | `path="§46.6-10\|(A)(2)"` |
| "§ 46.7-10" | `path="§46.10"` | `path="§46.7-10"` |
| "§ 46.6-12" | `path="§46.12"` | `path="§46.6-12"` |

- **Canonical:** p.2 line 19 reads "§ 46.6-10(A)(2), *but see* § 46.7-10" — so
  `46.7-10` is one section number, not `46.10`.
- **Basis for the new values:** the same file's correct siblings
  (`§46.6-10|(A)(1)`, `§46.6-10|(A)(2)`, `§46.4-2|(D)`) establish the shape.
- **Still open:** the underlying *citefinder* behaviour that emits adjacent
  `<cite>` pairs is unchanged — 8 such pairs remain (7 in this file, 1 in
  `2019/2.xml`), including the reader-visible "Chapter 1§1.9(G)(4)" fusion. That
  is a platform fix, not an XML fix.

## 11. Duplicated paragraphs (D-05)

Opinions are cited **by paragraph number**, so a duplicate shifts every later ¶
against the canonical.

### 2023 SMTCA 1 — ¶25–27 deleted (31 → 28 paragraphs)

¶22–24 were reproduced as ¶25–27 (~2,600 characters). The canonical states the
abuse-of-discretion standard **once** (p.11, read directly), running through the
*McCallister* cite straight to "In the case at hand, the matter is remanded…"
and then to VI. CONCLUSION.

**Which copy was kept, and why it matters:** the copies were *not* identical.
¶22–24 is strictly the better text — real `doc=` targets where ¶25 had
`doc="UNKNOWN"`, pin cites (`path=`) where ¶27 had none, and it retains the
footnote reference to note 3 that ¶27 lacked. Deleting the degraded copy also
removed 3 `doc="UNKNOWN"` placeholders as a side effect.

Paragraph count now matches the canonical's 28 exactly; all ¶ renumbered `1..28`.

### 2002 SMTCA 1 — duplicate ¶14 deleted (19 → 18 paragraphs)

¶14 and ¶15 were the same sentence and quotation. Canonical p.6 has it once.

**Which copy was kept:** ¶15, because it cites the **current** code
(`Stockbridge-Munsee Tribal Code` / `§TCT.01.03|(b)`) as the recodification pass
intends, whereas ¶14 cited the superseded `Old Stockbridge-Munsee Tribal Code`
(`§1.3|(B)`). All ¶ renumbered `1..18`.

## Not fixed — and why

**Needs source text we do not have (the 3 highest-severity findings).** These
cannot be fixed by editing; the missing pages have to come from the partner.
- **D-01** 2010 SMTCA 1 — a 3-page CONCURRENT OPINION (canonical pp.3–5) is
  unpublished. Its own last page ends mid-sentence and the fax header reads
  `P:5/9`, so **the scan itself is incomplete** — request the complete original
  before anything is published.
- **D-02** 2010 SMTCA 2 — both Declarations of Unanimity (pp.11–12) unpublished,
  and only 1 of the 3 caption-named judges recorded.
- **D-03** 2006 SMTCA 1 — the Scope of Review section (the court's standard of
  review) is absent. Restoring it should be paired with correcting the ¶7
  jurisdiction cite from §23(I) back to §23 — doing that cite fix alone would
  leave the opinion citing a provision that appears nowhere in it.
- **D-06** 2002 SMTCA 1 / 2002 SMTCA 2 — concurring judges (Martin, Butterfield)
  on canonical pp.7–8 unrecorded.

**Needs your legal/editorial judgment.**
- **D-22** — 13 citations rewritten from canonical docket numbers to reporter
  strings, losing pin cites and carrying fabricated dates. Fixing these means
  restoring the court's own citation text, which is a drafting decision about
  what the published opinion should say. (The *root* date error is fixed above.)
- **D-19** — 19 remaining `doc="UNKNOWN"` placeholders, all out-of-jurisdiction
  tribal decisions. Each needs a target identified or the `<cite>` wrapper
  dropped; the ledger lists the identified targets.
- **D-26** — canonical errors the XML faithfully reproduces, and three it
  silently corrects. The corpus currently applies two policies at once; that
  needs one decision, not a patch.
- **D-10 / D-24** — case-number punctuation normalization and the 45 dead
  reporter URLs. Both are single systemic policy calls.
- **D-25** — 31 `proof="needed"` markers **retained deliberately**. They are open
  proofing flags and several sit on exactly the cites touched here; clearing them
  is a sign-off, not a fix. They do not leak into published HTML.

**Needs a platform change, not an XML edit.**
- **D-28** — generated DOCX omits the `<parties>` caption; in 13 opinions a
  party's name appears nowhere in the document. The generator deliberately skips
  `meta` (documented in `docx/generators/base.py`), which is right for the panel
  but wrong for parties. Fix belongs in the generator.
- **D-04 (root cause)** — the citefinder emitting adjacent `<cite>` pairs.
- **D-11 (structural half)** — `<appeal_panel_judges>` / `<opinion_author>` are
  populated in only 1 of 46 opinions.
- **D-08** — no schema home for the canonical document-type label
  ("DENIAL OF APPEAL", "ORDER REMANDING CASE", …), absent in 40+ opinions.
- **D-12 / D-20** — list prose stored in `<heading>`, empty container headings,
  a mis-nested DECISION container, and two empty ¶ shells. Structural authoring
  work, deliberately not bundled with text corrections.
- **D-27** — `reconciled_case_numbers.json` has a duplicate `AP-2008-AA-0003` key
  that resolves to 2012 SMTCA 2 (the opinion slated for unpublication), silently
  dropping 2013 SMTCA 3's mapping.
